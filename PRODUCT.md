# SpiritByte legal website

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Product Purpose

Public Spanish and English privacy and terms pages for SpiritByte Android, requested for its Google Play submission. Developer: Octa_Dev. Public contact: oscar@octa-dev.com, confirmed by the owner. Repository: https://github.com/OscarTired/SpiritByte-Terms, supplied by the owner.

## Users

Android app users and Google Play reviewers who need to understand data handling and usage conditions.

## Capabilities and Constraints

All legal content must be delivered as static HTML, without authentication, location restrictions, or a dependency on JavaScript. Root URLs serve Spanish; /en/ URLs serve full English translations. An always-visible language switch retains the current document, and matching section IDs retain an anchor with JavaScript. Hosting targets GitHub Pages. Adding the policy inside the Android app remains a separate step.

## Brand Commitments

The user explicitly requested the same theme as the Android app. Preserve SpiritByte branding, its fox and Geist Pixel typography; use the app's default Amber CRT appearance. The app's other palettes may be offered as reading preferences.

## Evidence on Hand

Android source, README, manifest, dependencies, appearance screenshot and font license from D:/SpiritByte-Android. Existing web font from D:/SpireByte-V2/public/fonts. The current app does not declare Internet permission or telemetry dependencies. Vault and backup behavior is local.

## Product Principles

- Describe actual application behavior and avoid absolute security guarantees.
- Distinguish local data from developer collection, chosen backup providers and website hosting.
- Keep privacy, deletion and contact information easy to find.
- Prefer readable legal content over decorative effects.
