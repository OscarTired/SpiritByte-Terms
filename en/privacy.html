Before you begin

Your credentials are processed on your device. Octa_Dev does not receive your vault, does not have your keys and cannot recover your secrets. If you export a backup, you choose where to save it.

Developer and scope

Octa_Dev is the developer of SpiritByte Android (application ID com.spiritbyte.android) and the contact responsible for privacy communications relating to the application. You can reach us at oscar@octa-dev.com.

This policy describes how the Android application handles data and, separately, how this information website does so. The application is a local password manager: it does not require an account with Octa_Dev, has no synchronization servers, and does not request Internet access permission.

Data handled by the application

The application processes the information needed to manage your vault on your device. Keeping data locally does not mean that it is not sensitive.

Local information and its purpose
InformationPurpose
Credentials and notesTitles, usernames, passwords, website addresses, notes and favorites you enter to view and manage them.
Folders and metadataNames, hierarchy, icons, colors, record identifiers and dates used to organize the vault.
Master password and recoveryProtecting and unlocking the vault, and enabling local recovery with a 12-word phrase. They are not sent to Octa_Dev.
Preferences and imagesSaving your chosen appearance. If you select a background image or GIF, the application saves a local copy to display it.
Backup filesImporting and exporting encrypted backups you select through the Android document picker.

Vault data and preferences are stored in the application's private storage. SpiritByte does not access your location, contacts, microphone or camera to provide these features.

Collection and third parties

Octa_Dev does not collect or receive the contents of your vault through SpiritByte Android. The version described in this policy does not include advertising, trackers, usage analytics or SDKs that send crash reports. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

The application does not automatically transmit your credentials to third parties. Android and Google Play may process distribution, security or diagnostic information according to their own settings and policies; this is separate from processing by SpiritByte.

If you choose to send a support email, we will receive your email address and the content you include. We will use it to address your inquiry, not to access your vault. Do not send passwords, recovery phrases or files containing secrets.

Encryption and security

The local engine uses Argon2id for key derivation and XChaCha20-Poly1305 for authenticated encryption of the vault and backups. The application locks when it goes into the background and after two minutes of inactivity; it also restricts screenshots and excludes its data from Android automatic backups.

Data must be decrypted in memory to be displayed and edited while the vault is unlocked. We do not claim guaranteed erasure of all data in memory or absolute protection against compromised devices, malicious software or physical access to an unlocked device.

Choose a strong master password, keep your device updated and store the recovery phrase in a safe place.

Backups and files you choose

Exporting creates a .spiritbyte file encrypted with the backup password you choose. The file is encrypted before being passed to the Android document picker. The vault recovery phrase cannot unlock an exported backup.

You choose the destination: device storage or a provider available in the Android picker. If you choose a cloud service, that provider will receive the encrypted file and apply its own privacy policy. This is not automatic SpiritByte synchronization.

Exported copies remain in their destinations even if you delete records or uninstall the application. You must delete them separately, including copies on other devices, services or in trash folders. Backgrounds and visual preferences are not part of the vault backup.

Clipboard

When you use the option to copy the recovery phrase, that information is placed on the Android clipboard. It is marked as sensitive content on supported versions to limit its preview.

The clipboard is a system feature and may be exposed depending on the Android version, permissions and installed applications. Avoid pasting sensitive information into untrusted applications and clear the clipboard after using it. SpiritByte does not guarantee automatic clipboard clearing.

Retention and deletion

Local content is retained while you keep the application's data on your device. You can edit or delete a credential from its screen in the vault. Deleting a folder does not delete the credentials it contains.

  1. To delete all local information: open Android Settings → Apps → SpiritByte → Storage → Clear data or Clear storage. You can also uninstall the application. The names of these options vary between devices.
  2. To delete an imported background: use the “Delete saved image” option in the appearance settings (called “Eliminar imagen guardada” in the Spanish app interface).
  3. To delete backups: delete each exported file from the destination where you saved it, including copies and trash folders.

Clearing the application's data may cause irreversible loss of access to your vault if you do not have a usable backup. Logical deletion does not guarantee that the device or an external provider physically overwrites every copy of the file.

There is no user account with Octa_Dev that you need to request to delete. We also do not maintain a central copy of your vault that we can delete or recover.

Support emails are retained for the time needed to address and resolve the inquiry, including related follow-up. You can request their deletion by writing to our contact email; if a legal obligation requires us to retain specific information, we will inform you of the applicable reason.

Privacy inquiries and rights

You can request information, correction or deletion of contact data you have voluntarily sent us, and exercise any rights available to you under applicable law, by writing to oscar@octa-dev.com.

Because we do not receive vault data, viewing, editing, exporting and deleting its contents takes place on your device. You do not need to share your secrets for us to address a privacy inquiry.

Use by minors

SpiritByte Android is a credential management tool and is not specifically designed for children. We do not request users' ages or collect children's data through the application. If a minor uses the application, their parent or guardian should supervise its use and the safekeeping of keys.

If you believe a minor has sent us personal data by email, contact us so we can review its deletion.

Privacy on this website

This site does not include its own forms, advertising, analytics or trackers, and does not set its own cookies. If you change the color palette, we save that preference in your browser's local storage. You can remove it by clearing the site's data; it is not sent to Octa_Dev.

When this site is hosted on GitHub Pages, GitHub may record technical visit information, including IP addresses, to provide and protect its service. See GitHub's privacy statement. This section will be updated if the hosting provider changes.

Email links open your email client; we only receive information if you choose to send us a message. This website cannot access the contents of your Android vault.

Changes to this policy

We will update this policy when features or data handling change. The date at the start of this document identifies the published version. Any future feature that transmits data must be explained in this policy and in the application, with the appropriate disclosures and permissions, before it is used.
